# Support

Public support information is maintained at
<https://openlyuseful.org/support>.

For usage questions, reproducible defects, and feature proposals, use the issue
tracker linked from the relevant project page:

- Project Status: <https://github.com/Openly-Useful/project-status>
- RunGlance: <https://github.com/Openly-Useful/project-status>

For security issues, follow [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md) and contact
`hello@openlyuseful.org` privately with a `[Security]` subject tag. General
questions may use the same address with a `[Support]` subject tag.

Support is currently community and best-effort. There is no hosted service,
paid plan, uptime promise, or service-level agreement. Marketplace and
commercial support activation remains pending the formation of Openly Useful
LLC and separate publication approval.
